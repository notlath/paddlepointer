# 06 — Consent wording and privacy notice approved by MTC's DPO

**What to build:** MTC's Data Protection Officer or legal team approves three things (ADR 0006):
- the checkbox wording
- the privacy notice it links to: what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to withdraw
- how long Lead data is kept

The approved wording gets a version label, e.g. `2026-10-v1`. The app stores that label with each consent and shows the approved wording in place of ticket 03's placeholder. Decide where the privacy notice lives: an existing mtc.com.ph privacy page, or a new one.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** needs-human

- [ ] Retention period decided and recorded in ADR 0006's consequences
- [ ] Version label chosen and set in the app
