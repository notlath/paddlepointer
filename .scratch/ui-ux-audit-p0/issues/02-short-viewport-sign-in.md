# 02 — Keep every sign-in portal usable on short viewports

**What to build:** Admins, players, and visitors can reach every required field, primary action, portal switch, and explanatory message on small phones and short landscape screens. Content reflows or scrolls instead of being clipped by the viewport.

Origin: UI/UX audit finding U-01.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Establish an accessible visual baseline

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Admin, Player, and Visitor sign-in portals show every required control at 320×568 and 375×667
- [ ] The same portals remain fully operable at 667×375 landscape size
- [ ] The primary sign-in action and available portal-switch actions can always be reached by scrolling
- [ ] Opening an on-screen keyboard does not permanently hide the focused input or submit action
- [ ] Enlarging text to 200% does not clip content or trap the page in a non-scrollable region
- [ ] Desktop sign-in composition and branding remain intact
