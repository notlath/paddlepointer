# 08: Event and Court Analytics subject

**What to build:** Recreate Event and Court Analytics in the native Analytics preview so organisers can compare Events, understand court utilization and inspect duration quality through PaddlePoint KPIs, custom charts, filters and paginated details.

**Blocked by:** 02: Native analytics session and selection shell.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The subject presents the approved hero and supporting KPIs with a maximum of four meaningful values.
- [ ] Event and court comparison charts use governed Qlik dimensions and measures and participate in the shared selection state.
- [ ] Court utilization and Event details are native, paginated and accessible rather than embedded Qlik tables.
- [ ] Missing-duration information remains visible in context or detail and is not promoted into a KPI tile.
- [ ] Event and court filters expose associative possible, alternative and excluded states.
- [ ] One runnable automated check covers utilization mapping, bounded paging and a filter selection round trip.
